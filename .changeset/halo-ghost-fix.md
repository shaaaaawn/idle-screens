Glow halos no longer ghost. Shell push was proportional to the glow PART's bounding sphere, and the crystal-finned breeds carry a glow part larger than their body — the shells became a displaced double of the whole fish, visible on the wall as a smeared shadow. Push is now quoted against the whole model (~1.5%/3.5%, under one voxel, so cube-normal face separation is subpixel), and a whole-silhouette glow part gets a single faint veil instead of a bright double.
