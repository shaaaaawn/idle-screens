The farm, in-house (`./farm`): a static 512-entry asset-CID manifest plus breed ranges, trait vocabulary and pure URL builders — `fishAssets(85)` resolves every asset for any minted token with no AWS call, no metadata round-trip and no third-party resolver on the path to a frame. `fishMix` now accepts **any** token id 1–512 (`"2,85,124,234"`), not just the six curated entries, and knows the eight designed-but-unminted breeds (blowfish, hackerfish, glowfish, babyfish, shark, crab, jellyfish, dori) for future tank life.
