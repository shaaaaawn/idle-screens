Floor covers (`floorCover`): what the village lays on its floor. `lawn` mows a striped (or checked) lawn into every home's yard; `mosaic` tiles the square in a compass rose round the fountain (stone, shell or sea-glass); `mat` puts a woven doormat at every door; `steps` lays stepping stones down the paths; `ripples` combs the open sand into ripples across the current. Lawns, mosaic and ripples are painted by the floor's shader (any terrain, no triangles); mats and stones are cubes, on flat ground only. Current socks now stream down one shared current, the one the ripples follow.
