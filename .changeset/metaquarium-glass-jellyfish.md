Jellyfish are glass. The flotilla is drawn three times from one geometry — the lit cores opaque, the shells into depth only, then the shells' colour blended — so a bell is see-through to its own lantern and to the water (and the fish) behind it, but never to its own inner voxel faces. Face-on it is nearly clear, at a grazing edge solid and bright, lit from inside (more on the squeeze), with a faint film of colour in the rim; lines stay nearly solid and the far giants fainter. Behaviour: each jelly is thrown upward by its squeeze and sinks until the next (a quick rise, a long fall), and beats at its own rate — small bells quicker, the far giants slow. Two extra draw calls for the whole sky.
