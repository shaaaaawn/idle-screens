Pyramids: `pyramids` (0–1) raises voxel pyramids round the tank.
- **Size:** they are cut on the fish's own grid. One voxel is FISH_LENGTH/14, so a step of the stair is a classic token's pixel.
- **Symmetry:** every one is exactly symmetric. It has an odd base, a single-cube apex, and paint keyed on the sorted |x| and |z| of each cell.
- **Placement:** many stand far off in the haze, past the fog (hazed as the horizon is). Some stand in the middle distance and a few small ones by the glass.
- **Shapes:** Giza (51°), step, bent, Djoser's six tiers, Mayan (stairs up every face, a temple on top), wireframes of light, and floating octahedra.
- **Stones:** sandstone, limestone, obsidian, jade, gold, lapis, coral, crystal, nacre, basalt. The room chooses the stone unless `pyramidMix` names some.
- **Details:** glyph friezes, coral and moss on the treads, veins of light, gilded or glowing capstones that light the floor, and an eye on some.
- **How did they get there:** standing, half sunk, leaning where they settled, buried apex-first, floating, or missing a capstone that lies in the sand beside it.
- **Cost:** two draws in all.
