`ambientOcclusion` (0–1, default 0, high tier): crevices and contact points — between voxels, under ledges, where a fish or a lamp meets the sand — take less of the water's light, from the frame's own depth (the buffer depth of field already keeps). Opposite pairs of taps, so a floor seen at a slant never darkens itself.
