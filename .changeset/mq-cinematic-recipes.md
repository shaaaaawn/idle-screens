Two recipes that show the polished look in one argument: `reef-cinematic` (a small reef cast framed close by `shot: cast`, soft shafts, fish over their own shadows, rim and highlights) and `night-cinematic` (glowfish and baby fish under jellyfish lanterns in the deep, a shark sliding through, every outline caught in a rim of light). Both build on the new `highlights`, `raySoftness`, `nearFade`, `fishRim`, `fishShadows` and `shot: cast`.
