Comic dolphins (experimental): `dolphinCast` gives the dolphins in a mix their own characters, in order — `showman` (a navy suit, a white shirt-front, a red tie; quick and big), `drifter` (sandy and matte, a red trucker cap, heavy lids; slow, slouched, leaning), `bigguy` (big, slate grey, a dark tee; small deadpan gestures). Each is still a dolphin: its skin and finish recoloured, a shirt tinted into its chest, a prop riding a bone, and an act (tempo, size of gesture, slouch, lean, lids) its frames read. Stage types, never anyone's likeness.
