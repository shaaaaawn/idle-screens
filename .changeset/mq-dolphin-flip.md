The dolphin's flips and rolls are fluid. The whole turn was baked into a bone, and when the tank blended the clip (against the swim as a moment faded in and out, or under a vignette gesture's envelope, Dot's hop) a turn past half-way interpolated the short way, so the dolphin unwound mid-flip. The clips now keep the curl, tuck and flippers; the turn is one smooth closed-form angle (`dolphinTurn`) that the tank applies to the whole dolphin about its body, played once and never blended.
