The dolphin's tail reads as a dolphin's: the tail stock is a keel (one cube thin, four deep) that runs into the flukes instead of a round thin stalk, the taper starts at the dorsal, and the flukes are sized to a real bottlenose (span ~0.29 of the body, swept ~31°) instead of 0.39. Tests measure the shipped model against the animal: the keel's section, a smooth taper, the fluke span, and the stroke (fluke-tip heave ~0.2 of the body, the head near still).
