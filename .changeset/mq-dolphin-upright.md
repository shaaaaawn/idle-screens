The dolphin acts. In a vignette it stands on its tail for character beats — talk, nod, bow, shake, wiggle, peek, rest — as real dolphins do (a spy-hop) and cartoon ones talk: nose up, its head tipped toward whoever it plays to, its front to its partner or about 60° to you so its eye and smile read, its flukes sculling below. It stays standing while it stays put (a comic listening to a heckle), and lies down to swim to its next mark or do a dolphin thing (hop, spin, the dances). Also: in a hard turn its head now curls belly-ward with its tail (its sign was backwards).
