A dolphin: Dot's friend (experimental). Drawn in-house in the designer's voxel style (breeds/rig/dolphin-model.mjs) in colours of its own: a blue-grey cape over pale-blue flanks and a cream belly, a short beak under a round melon, a smile that turns up at the corner, big eyes on the sides of its head with a blush under each, a blowhole, a low hooked dorsal fin and FLAT flukes — so it reads as a dolphin, never a shark. Rigged in Blender (breeds/rig/dolphin.py): one soft body that strokes up and down as a dolphin does, a jaw that chatters, flippers, a blowhole, eyes that blink and squint, pupils with catchlights on the outer face of each eye. src/dolphin.ts swims it: strokes phased by the distance swum, coasting glides, hovering, bursts, and a personality (playful, chatty, curious, show-off) that picks its moments — chatter, nod, shake, wave, bubble (it lets a bubble go from its blowhole), spin and flip — slowing for them and turning an eye to you for the friendly ones; its pupils look at you, ahead, up after its bubble, and at its friend in a vignette, where it plays talk, nod, hop, spin, wiggle and peek with its own body. Castable as `dolphin` / id 613.
