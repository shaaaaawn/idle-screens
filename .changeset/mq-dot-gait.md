Dot's arms and legs keep step at any hour. As a vignette actor its walk and run took their phase from `t × speed`, so whenever the script eased its pace the legs raced, faster the longer the screen had been running (hours into a channel they whirled). They now follow the distance the script has actually walked it (`actorTravel`, a per-script table over one loop, a closed form). A turn on the spot now always steps forward with the walk (it stepped forward then back, and used the run cycle after a run). The glitch shiver is ~5 Hz instead of ~15 Hz, a body shaking rather than a blur.
