Dot, the first CHARACTER (experimental): Geode Hollow's courier, castable as `dot` (id 612). A box on two legs whose face is a screen, in a mail cap with a glowing antenna, blue sleeves and white gloves, red sneakers, a mailbag on its back and a little fish tail — drawn in the designer's voxel style but in fixed colours (every part `PAINT-#rrggbb`; a `SCREEN-…#rrggbb` material keeps its own phosphor). Rigged in Blender (`breeds/rig/dot.py`): hips, a torso that bows, arms with elbows and wrists, legs posed by two-bone IK that never let a sole under the floor (checked over every frame of every clip at build time), a cap and antenna that follow through; 29 clips — walk, run, sneak, idle, talk, nod, shake, hop, wiggle, wave, cheer, rest, spin, think, bow, deliver, point, glitch, skid, clap, shrug, facepalm, laugh, salute, search, stretch, jump for joy, trip and the twist. `src/dot.ts` walks it on the floor the crab's way — its own route, walk/run/sneak bouts, feet in step with the ground, its stance lifted so no foot sinks into a curved slope — and gives it 18 moments, turning to you for the friendly ones, each with a face on its screen (moods, blinks, a question then an idea, a letter, stars, a laugh, a glitch and reboot; new glyphs). In a vignette it is an actor on its feet, playing talk, nod, shake, hop, spin, wiggle, peek, bow and rest with its own clips (talking flaps a mouth on its screen). New recipe `dot-delivers`.
