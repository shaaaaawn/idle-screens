`fishAO` (0–1, default 0): a fish's own corners darken — the root of a fin against the flank, the studs on a blowfish, the steps of a shark's side, the joins in a snail's shell — so a fish reads as a solid thing rather than as panels of flat colour. The cubes are read back off each breed's voxel faces in its bind pose (the parts split by material count as one body; Draco's jitter is snapped to the voxel step), baked once per breed the first frame it is on, and applied to the lit coats' surface colour, so it holds on every tier.
