`fishRim` (0–1, default 0): a thin edge of the water's light round each fish's silhouette (lit mode), brighter on top and dimmed with a follow-spot's house lights — what separates a dark fish from dark water. The fresnel runs on each mesh's envelope (an ellipsoid fitted to its box), not its voxel faces, so it outlines the fish instead of every cube step.
