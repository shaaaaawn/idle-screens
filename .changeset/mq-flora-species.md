Flora: eight new species (anemone, staghorn, brain coral, sea whip, barrel sponge, glow caps, sea pen and a giant clam, at most two to a garden), per-plant colour instead of one teal, the odd giant kelp and old fan, and a `floraMix` param (`kelp:3, whip, seapen`). With `floraMix` empty the environment picks the garden: coral on a reef, kelp and whips in the kelp forest, tube worms and glow caps at the vent. Existing scenes with `floraDensity` > 0 grow a different, more varied garden. The shoal now keeps above every plant, not only the lit ones.
