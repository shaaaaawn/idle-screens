Metaquarium: `followAvoid` keeps the followed fish in view. When a home, a castle wall, a lamp or a kelp stand comes between the chase camera and its fish, the camera rises a little over low clutter and otherwise pulls in toward the fish, like a game camera's spring arm. It never comes inside the fish's-eye threshold and never booms through the surface. Pure and continuous (no state, no snaps): a frame is still a function of t. 0 (the default) is the plain chase camera.
