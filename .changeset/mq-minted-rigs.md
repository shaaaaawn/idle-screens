The minted breeds get rigs, as the other breeds have. Each breed is one model, so one rig in Blender animates every token of it.

- **Angelfish** (all 200 tokens): the tail beats, and the tall dorsal and anal fins sway, with their streamers trailing on a longer lag. An angelfish glides, sometimes shows off with its fins raised and spread, and sometimes darts with its fins folded back.
- **Sea turtles** (all 16): until now they glided rigid. Now they fly on their front flippers, down and back on the power stroke and feathered on the way up. They glide with flippers swept back, turn their heads to look about, and hang in place sculling.
