`nearFade` (0–1, default 0): a creature that comes too close to the camera dissolves instead of being sliced by the lens — an ordered 4×4 dither by view depth, so the fish stays opaque (no sorting) and a frame is still a function of t. At 1 a fish is whole beyond about a fish and a half; the follow camera's own subject never fades.
