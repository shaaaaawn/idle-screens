The bundled jellyfish is removed. It was a draft model and isn't part of the cast any more: its GLB, its lazy chunk and its catalog entry are gone. `fishMix: "jellyfish"` and id 607 are now unknown, so they're reported and dropped like any misspelt breed, and the rest of the scene plays. The other bundled breeds keep their ids (601–606, 608). The jellyfish lanterns of `skyLanterns` are separate built-in models and are unchanged.
