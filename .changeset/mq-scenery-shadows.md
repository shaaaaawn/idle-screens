`sceneryShadows` (0–1, default 0): a soft contact shadow pooled under everything that stands on the floor — rocks, homes, the castle, pyramids, wild geodes, lamps, the town's props — laid on the slope it stands on, so scenery stands on the sand instead of floating over it. One instanced draw per world, built the first frame it is on; `Scenery.footprints` lists what stands where.
