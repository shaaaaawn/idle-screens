A snail, our third in-house breed and a floor creature (`snail`, id 611; experimental). Drawn voxel by voxel in the designer's style (`breeds/rig/snail-model.mjs`): a big round shell in the fish's coat with a spiral on each flank, a sea-green body with a smile, eyes on stalks, and two feelers whose tips glow. Rigged in Blender (`breeds/rig/snail.py`): a rigid shell over a soft body that stretches and draws in without cracking, stalks that roll in, blinking eyes. `src/snail.ts` drives it the crab's way: it glides the floor very slowly at its own pace (the wave down its foot keeps time with the ground), and stops to look about, turn and look at you, graze, stretch up tall, or pull into its shell and peek back out. Its eyes are its own: big 3×3 eyes on the stalks, each pupil one sliding quad with a catchlight, aimed every frame — at you when it stops to look, down at its food when it grazes, ahead as it glides, about in saccades — and they blink and squint (the shared `eyeLife` display leaves them be). A new `snailShell` param dresses the shells: `banded` (the coat and spiral as drawn), `pearl` (iridescent nacre), `gold` (polished metal), `glow` (a dark shell whose spiral breathes light, a 7 s breath); `mix` (default) gives each snail its own, and a live steer takes on the next frame. New recipes `snail-garden` and `snail-night`.
