`stoneStyle` (`classic` default | `weathered`): the world's stone — rocks, arches, the geode homes and the castle keep. `weathered` is river-grey: the water's ambient keeps faces turned from the key light out of the black, each facet takes its own shade (a hash of where it is, so no seeded layout moves), and silt settles on the tops — so stone in a lit village reads as stone instead of a hole in the picture.
