`voxelAO` (0–1, default 0): the plants' cubes darken where they meet each other and the sand, the corner shading every voxel renderer has, so a coral reads as solid instead of as flat cut-outs. It is baked once, the first frame it is on, and applied to the vertex colour, so unlike the screen-space passes it holds on every tier, the basic one included.

Inside a geode room, a lining crystal whose nearest point comes within a few units of the lens now dissolves whole. Bedtime's dollhouse camera had one of the long "great points" filling a third of the frame, flat purple, the whole piece long. The wall seen from anywhere else is unchanged.
