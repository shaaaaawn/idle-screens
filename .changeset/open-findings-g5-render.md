Renderer fixes for live-vs-intent divergences. `rect.feather` paints one smooth separable falloff instead of six stepped nested fills (X383); a still or drifting feathered rect is pre-rendered once per entity (rotation baked in) and copied 1:1, so it costs less than the old fills, and feathered rects with `alpha < 1` now draw at their authored alpha (the old fills over-composited the core). A `taper` stroke fills its outline once, so a wide translucent mark no longer beads at every segment joint (X386). `trail` echoes of shaped sprites (polygon, stroke, rect, ring, streak, bar) draw the sprite's own shape at the echo's time instead of discs (X387). A hard circle's trail is one continuous tapering ribbon instead of spaced dots (X469), falling back to its echo discs when a layer's ribbons would cover more than a tenth of the screen; soft-circle trails gap-fill within a small per-layer budget. Frame time on a 4×-throttled CPU stays within ~10 % of the previous renderer for trailed and feathered scenes, apart from a single fast ribbon (+1.5 ms) and colour-gliding feathered rects (+1.7 ms with 30 rects). Perception samples trails through the same sampler.
