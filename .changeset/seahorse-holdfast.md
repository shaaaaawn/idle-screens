Seahorses hold on. Every so often a minted seahorse pauses its route, swims to the nearest stalk of kelp or sea whip, wraps its tail round it and sways with the plant: feeding, looking about, then letting go and picking up where it stopped. A `@pair` of seahorses link tails instead, facing each other and leaning back into the heart courting seahorses make. A hunter or a shy one holds longest. The rig gains a `grip` dial that wraps the tail's last three bones into a loop, an `aim` dial that swings the loop to either side, and a `grasp` node at its centre that the tank pins to the stem. Flora now records the kelp and whip stalk cells, each with its own sway. The tank exposes `stemAt`, a JS mirror of the plant sway shader, and `inspect()` reports each hold's kind, phase and pin error. A plant's dimorphic small form now shrinks its body cells along with its vertices.
